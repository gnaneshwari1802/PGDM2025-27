# Q&A prep: Financial Inclusion and Digital Payments (PMJDY, UPI)

**Key numbers to remember (all verified 12 Nov 2026)**
- PMJDY (19 Aug 2026): 59.09 cr accounts; 45.95 cr rural/semi-urban (77.8%); 32.92 cr women (55.7%); deposits ₹3,16,514 cr (~₹5,356/account); 41.29 cr RuPay cards. Launched 28 Aug 2014. Source: PIB, "12 Years of PMJDY", 27 Aug 2026.
- RBI FI-Index: 67.0 (Mar 2025) → 70.0 (Mar 2026).
- UPI Oct 2026: 25.47 bn txns (+23% YoY), ₹31.53 lakh cr (+16% YoY), ~822 mn/day. Apr 22.35 bn → Oct 25.47 bn. Banks live: 741 (Jul 2026).
- Average ticket in Oct 2026 ≈ ₹31.53 lakh cr ÷ 25.47 bn ≈ ₹1,238.

**Likely questions**
1. *What is the JAM Trinity?* Jan Dhan (account) + Aadhaar (identity/e-KYC) + Mobile (authentication and access). It lets subsidies go straight to beneficiary accounts (DBT).
2. *Does having an account mean inclusion?* No. Dormant or zero-balance accounts are the usual critique. Usage matters, which is why the FI-Index measures access, usage and quality.
3. *How does UPI work?* VPA/QR, then NPCI switch routing, then UPI PIN, then the remitter bank debits and the beneficiary bank credits. Real-time, 24x7, push payment. The merchant never sees your card or account number.
4. *UPI vs IMPS/NEFT/RTGS?* UPI is for retail P2P/P2M. IMPS is a real-time interbank rail that UPI complements. NEFT is batch, for routine transfers. RTGS is for high-value transfers (₹2 lakh minimum).
5. *Is UPI really free? What is the MDR issue?* P2P is free. NPCI proposed MDR of 0.4% (capped at ₹300) on select large-merchant payments above ₹2,000, and a flat ₹5 for some categories. About 95% of P2M is exempt. Rollout was deferred until after Diwali, with a new date still to be announced. Sources disagree on some details, so state it as "proposed".
6. *Who pays for UPI then?* The government has used incentive schemes to compensate banks and PSPs. Debate on sustainability is why MDR is being discussed.
7. *Biggest risks?* Fraud (phishing, fake QR), concentration (a few apps hold most volume), downtime, and the digital divide. Mitigations: UPI Lite (₹1,000 per txn, ₹5,000 wallet), UPI 123PAY for feature phones, and RBI grievance norms.
8. *Why AePS and Bank Mitras?* They give a last-mile channel for people without smartphones, via Aadhaar biometrics.
9. *What would you measure next?* Share of active (not just opened) accounts, women's usage, credit access via transaction data, and rural digital transaction share.
